ABOUT AMERICAN LYCEUM

Education That Builds Tomorrow's Leaders

At American Lyceum International School (ALIS), we believe education is more than academic success. It is about developing confident, creative and responsible individuals who are prepared for the opportunities and challenges of tomorrow.

Our educational approach combines academic excellence with emotional well-being, physical education and innovative learning experiences. Through STEM and STEAM-based learning, we encourage students to think critically, explore ideas and develop practical skills.

Academic Excellence

Building strong foundations through quality teaching and learning.

STEM & STEAM Learning

Encouraging creativity, innovation and problem-solving skills.

Holistic Development

Supporting emotional well-being, physical health and strong character.

Future-Ready Skills

Preparing students for future challenges and opportunities.

LEARNING PATHWAYS

OUR PROGRAMS

Discover learning opportunities designed to support students at every stage of their educational journey.

Montessori / KG

Our Montessori program follows the EYFS curriculum, focusing on early childhood development through play-based learning. We nurture curiosity and creativity, laying a strong foundation for lifelong learning.

Read More

Primary

Our Primary program emphasizes the development of critical thinking skills. Through interactive lessons and hands-on activities, students learn to analyze, evaluate, and create, fostering intellectual growth.

Read More

Pre-Secondary

In Pre-Secondary, we build on foundational knowledge while introducing more complex concepts. Our curriculum encourages independent learning and problem-solving, preparing students for higher academic challenges.

Read More

Secondary

Our Secondary program offers a rigorous curriculum designed to prepare students for Matriculation and O-Levels. We focus on academic excellence and character-building activities, with experienced faculty guiding students through advanced subjects.

Read More

Matriculation

The Matriculation program at ALIS is tailored to achieve high grades. Our experienced faculty and comprehensive study plans ensure students excel in their board exams, paving the way for future success.

Read More

O-Levels

Our O-Levels program is designed to meet international standards. With a focus on academic excellence and experienced faculty, we prepare students to achieve straight A*s/As and excel in their future endeavors.
